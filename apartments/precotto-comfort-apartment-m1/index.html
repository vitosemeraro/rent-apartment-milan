A unique solution in Precotto: two independent flats connected by an internal door. Use them as one large home or as two separate apartments, with double of everything: two kitchens, two bathrooms, two living and sleeping areas. Space, privacy and flexibility for groups of up to 8, ideal for two families travelling together.

Each unit has a fully equipped kitchen, a modern bathroom with shower, a bright living area and a bedroom; there is also an entrance hall, a study corner with desk and a panoramic balcony over the trees. The M1 Precotto metro is 350 m away, for the city centre, Bicocca and the trade fairs at Rho Fiera. The second unit opens from the 6th guest.

A Superhost listing on Airbnb, rated 5.0: I take care of everything personally, from check-in to cleaning.
