Bocconi University sits in the south of Milan, between Porta Romana, Viale Bligny and the new Sarfatti campus. Demand for flats in the surrounding streets is very high, and so are prices. Here is how to choose the right area for your budget.

## The areas closest to Bocconi

- **Ripamonti and Bligny**: walking distance to campus, lively and student-oriented, among the most expensive areas for students.
- **Porta Romana and Lodi**: on the M3 yellow line, a few minutes from campus, good mix of shops and services.
- **Navigli**: beautiful and full of life in the evening, but noisy and pricey.

## A smarter alternative: live on the metro

Many Bocconi students rent further out and commute. From our apartments in **Precotto and Turro** (north-east Milan) you take the **M1** to Duomo and change to the **M3** to Porta Romana, or take a tram: around **35 minutes** in total. In exchange you get quieter streets, a whole apartment instead of a room, and a lower total monthly cost.

## Checklist before you sign

- Ask for a **registered contract**: in Italy students can sign a [university student lease](/transitional-lease-contract-milan/) (6–36 months) or a transitional lease for shorter periods.
- Compare the **total monthly cost**: rent, bills, Wi-Fi, agency fee and deposit.
- Never send money before seeing the contract and the landlord's identity.
