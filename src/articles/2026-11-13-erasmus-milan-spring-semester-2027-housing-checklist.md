## Which documents do I need before I sign?

1. **Passport or ID card.**
2. **Codice fiscale** (Italian tax code), from the Agenzia delle Entrate or the Italian consulate in your country. Most landlords need it to register the lease.
3. **Acceptance letter or Learning Agreement**, which proves the reason for your stay.
4. **European Health Insurance Card** if you are an EU citizen, or private health insurance if you are not.
5. **Non-EU students**: your study visa. After arrival you must apply for the residence permit (permesso di soggiorno) within 8 working days, and the registered lease is part of the file.

## Which lease should I ask for?

For a semester, ask for a **transitional lease** registered with the Agenzia delle Entrate, with your enrolment as the reason. You may need a copy for your residence permit or your grant. Our guide to the [transitional lease in Milan](/transitional-lease-contract-milan/) explains how it works. The Milan tourist tax does not apply to monthly contracts.

## How do I avoid housing scams?

- Never pay before you know who the host is and have seen the lease.
- Be wary of prices far below the area average.
- Ask for a registered lease and a receipt for every payment.
- Prefer a video call of the flat if you cannot visit.

**Can I rent for exactly one semester?** Yes. The minimum stay is 28 or 30 nights depending on the flat, so a five-month semester works.

**Do you ask for a deposit?** For monthly stays we do not ask for the usual deposit of up to three months' rent. Read more about [apartments in Milan with no deposit](/apartments-milan-no-deposit/).

**Can two Erasmus students share?** Yes, both flats sleep up to 2 people, so you can split the rent.

**Are bills extra?** No. Electricity, gas, heating, water and Wi-Fi are in the monthly price.
