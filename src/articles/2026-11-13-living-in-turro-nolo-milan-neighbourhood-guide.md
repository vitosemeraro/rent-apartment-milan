**Turro and NoLo** are neighbouring districts in north-east Milan on the **M1 red line**. Turro is quiet and residential, on the Naviglio Martesana; NoLo, just south towards Loreto, is livelier, with bars and a creative crowd. The Duomo is about 20 minutes away, and our furnished Turro studio costs **€1,300 a month**, bills included.

## Where exactly are Turro and NoLo?

Both sit on the M1 line between Loreto and Precotto. Going north from Loreto the stops are Pasteur, Rovereto, Turro, Gorla and Precotto.

- **NoLo** stands for "North of Loreto". It is the area around Viale Monza and Via Padova, roughly from Loreto to Rovereto.
- **Turro** starts around the M1 Turro stop and reaches the Naviglio Martesana, where it meets Gorla.
- **Precotto** is two stops further north, next to Bicocca.

If you want calm at night and NoLo's bars within a short walk, Turro is the middle ground.

## What is good about living in Turro?

- **The metro is close.** Our studio is a 2-minute walk from M1 Turro. Loreto (M2) is a few stops away, and Rho Fiera is reachable with no change on trains bound for Rho.
- **The Martesana is on the doorstep.** The canal path is 3 minutes from the studio, for running, cycling or walking towards Gorla and the centre.
- **Everyday shopping is easy.** There is a 24/7 supermarket, bars and a pharmacy right below the building.
- **It feels like a neighbourhood.** Fewer tourists than the centre, more locals, and free street parking is easier to find.

## What are the downsides? The honest part

- **Viale Monza is a busy road.** It carries heavy traffic. If you are sensitive to noise, choose a side street or a high floor. Our studio is on the 7th floor.
- **Few sights nearby.** Museums and monuments are in the centre, so plan on the M1 to Duomo for sightseeing.
- **NoLo can be loud at weekends.** The streets with bars stay busy late. That is great if you go out, less so if you sleep with the window open.
- **It changes block by block.** NoLo and Via Padova are mixed areas. Visit at different times of day, or ask us, before you decide.

## What can you do in the evening?

In NoLo you find bistros, bars and the **Zelig** cabaret on Viale Monza. Along the Martesana there are traditional trattorias with outdoor seating in summer. **Parco Trotter**, a former racecourse turned public park, hosts neighbourhood life and events. For more on parks and everyday life, read the [Precotto, Turro and Martesana neighbourhood guide](/precotto-turro-martesana-neighbourhood-guide/).

## FAQ

**Is Turro safe?**
Turro is a residential area with families, shops and the canal path. As in any city, use normal care at night, especially near busy roads and stations.

**Is NoLo a good place to live?**
It suits people who like a lively, mixed area with bars and small venues. If you want quiet, look at Turro or Precotto, still a short walk or metro ride away.

**Which metro serves Turro and NoLo?**
The M1 red line: Pasteur and Rovereto for NoLo, Turro for Turro. Loreto, with the M2, is the interchange to the south.
