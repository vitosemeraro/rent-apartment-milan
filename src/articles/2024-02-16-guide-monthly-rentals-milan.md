A month or more in Milan is the best way to live the city like a local: your own kitchen, your neighbourhood café, the evening walk along the canal. Here's how monthly rentals work.

## Rent directly from the host

Agencies in Milan often charge one month's rent plus VAT. Renting **directly from the host** removes that cost and gives you one person to talk to before and during your stay.

## What to expect

- **Minimum stay**: usually 28–30 nights.
- **Price**: one monthly amount, often with a monthly discount compared with the nightly rate.
- **Contract**: a registered transitional lease for stays over 30 days.
- **Bills**: check whether electricity, heating and Wi-Fi are included.
