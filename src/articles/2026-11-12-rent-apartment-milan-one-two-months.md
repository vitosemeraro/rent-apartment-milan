You pay one final cleaning, sign a registered lease and leave no three-month deposit. Both flats are a few minutes from the M1.

## What is included in the price?

- Electricity, gas, heating and water.
- Wi-Fi (gigabit fibre in the Via Val di Non flat).
- Full furniture, equipped kitchen, washing machine, bed linen and towels.
- A desk or work area.
- Self check-in.

You do not open utility contracts or buy plates and sheets. That is the main difference from a standard lease in Milan, where bills are paid separately.

## Which lease do I sign for one or two months?

For stays of a month or more we sign a **transitional lease registered** with the Agenzia delle Entrate. It also works as proof of address. Our guide to the [transitional lease in Milan](/transitional-lease-contract-milan/) explains how it works.

For stays shorter than a month, the Precotto comfort apartment below is booked per night. For longer stays, read our guide to [monthly rentals in Milan](/monthly-rentals-milan/).

## Is there a deposit?

For monthly stays in our apartments **we do not ask for the usual deposit of up to three months' rent**. Payment terms are written in the lease, so read them before you sign. More on the page about [apartments in Milan with no deposit](/apartments-milan-no-deposit/).

## Why rent for a month instead of booking a hotel?

A month in a Milan hotel is priced night by night and rarely gives you a kitchen, a washing machine or a desk. With a furnished flat you cook, wash clothes and work from home, and the price for the month is fixed in advance. For a project, an internship or a research visit of a few weeks, that is usually simpler to budget.

## How long does it take to reach the centre?

From M1 Precotto or Turro the Duomo is about 20 minutes with no change. Bicocca is about 15 minutes and the Politecnico 15 to 20 minutes with a change at Loreto. Read the [Precotto, Turro and Martesana neighbourhood guide](/precotto-turro-martesana-neighbourhood-guide/) for more.

## What should I prepare before I arrive?

1. Passport or ID card.
2. The Italian tax code (**codice fiscale**), needed to register the lease.
3. Your exact arrival and departure dates.
4. The reason for your stay (work, study, research), stated in the transitional lease.

## Do I pay the tourist tax?

The Milan tourist tax applies to short stays under 30 days. It does not apply with a registered monthly lease. On per-night bookings it is €9.50 per adult per night for the first 14 nights.

Pick the apartment, check free dates in the booking calendar on its page and send your request: we reply directly, with no agency. If you have questions about the lease or the length of stay, write to us before you book.
