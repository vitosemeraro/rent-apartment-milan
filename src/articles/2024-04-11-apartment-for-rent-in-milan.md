Milan is Italy's capital of business, fashion and universities, and its rental market reflects it: demand is high and good apartments go quickly. These are the steps that make the search easier.

## 1. Choose the metro line, then the neighbourhood

Your daily commute matters more than the neighbourhood name. Look at the metro map first, then at the streets around the stations. **Brera** and **Navigli** are beautiful but expensive and noisy; north-east Milan on the **M1** (Precotto, Turro, Gorla) is quieter, cheaper and only about 20 minutes from the Duomo.

## 2. Compare the total monthly cost

Rent is only part of the cost. Add bills (electricity, gas, heating, water), Wi-Fi, the agency fee and the deposit, which in Milan is often 2–3 months. A slightly higher rent with **everything included** and **no deposit** can be cheaper overall.

## 3. Check what's in the apartment

For a stay of weeks or months you need a desk, fast Wi-Fi, a real kitchen and a washing machine. Ask for recent photos and the exact bed size.

## 4. Ask for a registered contract

Stays over 30 days require a lease registered with the tax office. It protects you and you need it for residence, permits and scholarships. [How the transitional lease works](/transitional-lease-contract-milan/).

## 5. Talk to the host before you book

A host who answers quickly before booking usually answers quickly during your stay too.
