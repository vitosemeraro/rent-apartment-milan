Looking for an **apartment near Politecnico di Milano**? Our two furnished flats are not in Città Studi, but on the red **M1** line in north-east Milan. From Turro or Precotto you ride to Loreto, change to the green **M2** and get off at Piola: about **15–20 minutes** to the Leonardo campus, bills included in one monthly price.

Politecnico di Milano is one of Europe's top universities for engineering, architecture and design, and many of its programmes are taught in English. This guide shows the exact route from each of our apartments, where it works well and where it doesn't, so you can decide before you pay anything.

## Where are the Politecnico campuses?

- **Leonardo (Città Studi)**: the main campus, around Piazza Leonardo da Vinci, served by the M2 green line (Piola and Lambrate stations).
- **Bovisa**: engineering and design schools in the north-west, served by the suburban railway (Milano Bovisa station).

Living right next to the Leonardo campus is convenient but expensive. Many students choose a well-connected area a few metro stops away instead.

The route is the same for both flats: M1 towards the centre, change at **Loreto**, then one stop on the M2 to **Piola**.

## Is it worth living on the M1 instead of in Città Studi?

It depends on what you need. Here is how the two options compare:

| | Città Studi (next to campus) | Turro / Precotto (our flats) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commute to Leonardo | on foot | about 15–20 minutes by metro |
| Bills | often on top of the rent | included in the monthly price |
| Other universities | Statale (Città Studi) | Bicocca in about 15 minutes too |

The M1 is also a direct line to Duomo, Cadorna and Rho Fiera, so the centre and the trade-fair district are easy to reach.

## What do students usually struggle with?

- **High deposits**: many landlords ask for 2–3 months up front, plus an agency fee.
- **Hidden costs**: electricity, gas, heating and Wi-Fi often come on top of the rent.
- **Contracts in Italian**: a lease you don't understand is a risk, and an unregistered one is useless for residence permits and scholarships.

## Do I need a deposit and which lease will I get?

- **No three-month deposit** for monthly stays ([more on renting with no deposit](/apartments-milan-no-deposit/)).
- **A registered contract**: a [transitional or student lease](/transitional-lease-contract-milan/) valid for residence and visa purposes.
- **Whole apartments**: a desk, a full kitchen and a washing machine, not a room in a shared flat.

## When should I start looking?

Start **6–8 weeks before** the semester begins. September and February are the busiest months: if you are coming for the autumn semester, book by July.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which metro stop is closest to the Politecnico Leonardo campus?**
Piola on the M2 green line. Lambrate, one stop further, is also close to part of the campus.

**Can I reach the Politecnico without changing lines?**
No. From Turro and Precotto you always change once, at Loreto, from the M1 to the M2. The change takes place inside the same station.

**Are your apartments good for Bovisa?**
Not really. The Bovisa campus is on the opposite side of the city, and the trip needs more changes than the Leonardo route.

**Can two students share the apartment?**
Yes. Both flats host up to 2 guests, and the monthly price is for the whole apartment.
