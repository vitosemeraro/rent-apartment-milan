Renting in Milan is expensive, and the market moves fast. If you are a student or a young professional, the key is to look beyond the most famous neighbourhoods and focus on what matters every day: the metro, the total monthly cost and a clear contract.

## Where affordable rentals are in Milan

The cheapest well-connected areas are along the metro lines outside the historic centre. **North-east Milan on the M1 red line** (Precotto, Turro, Gorla) is a good example: quiet residential streets, 24-hour supermarkets, the Naviglio Martesana for running and cycling, and the Duomo about 20 minutes away.
