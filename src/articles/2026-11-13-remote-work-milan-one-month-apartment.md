## Is the internet fast enough for video calls?

The Via Val di Non flat runs on a **gigabit fibre** line, the best choice if you spend the day on video calls or upload large files. The Turro studio and the Precotto apartment have Wi-Fi included in the price. If your work needs a specific upload speed, ask us before you book and tell us what you use it for.

## Where do I actually work?

- **Via Val di Non**: a dedicated workstation, air conditioning in the bedroom and kitchen, and a balcony with a city view for breaks. The bedroom is separate, so a partner can sleep while you take an early call.
- **Turro**: a work desk inside a bright studio on the 7th floor, with air conditioning. Good for one person.
- **Precotto**: built for groups of up to 8, with a big dining table. It suits a small team working together for a week or two more than a single remote worker.

## Which lease do I sign for a month?

For a month or more we sign a **transitional lease registered** with the Agenzia delle Entrate, with the reason for your stay (work, for example) written in it. The minimum stay is 30 nights in Turro and 28 nights in Via Val di Non. Our guide to the [transitional lease in Milan](/transitional-lease-contract-milan/) explains how it works.

## Do I need to pay a deposit?

For monthly stays **we do not ask for the usual deposit of up to three months' rent**, and there is no agency fee because you book directly with the host. Payment terms are written in the lease. More on [apartments in Milan with no deposit](/apartments-milan-no-deposit/).

## How far is the centre for a day out of the flat?

All three apartments are on the **M1 red line**. From Precotto or Turro the Duomo is about 20 minutes with no change, and San Babila about 15 minutes from Precotto. Bicocca is close to Via Val di Non. The same M1 goes straight to Rho Fiera if your month includes a trade fair. The [Precotto, Turro and Martesana neighbourhood guide](/precotto-turro-martesana-neighbourhood-guide/) covers shops, parks and the Martesana canal path for an evening walk or run.

## Why a flat instead of a hotel or a coworking package?

A hotel prices every night and rarely gives you a kitchen, a washing machine and a proper desk. With a furnished flat the month has one fixed price, you cook your own meals and you work from home without paying a coworking day pass. If you still want a coworking for meetings, the M1 gets you to the centre in about 20 minutes.

## What should I prepare before I arrive?

1. Passport or ID card.
2. The Italian tax code (**codice fiscale**), needed to register the lease.
3. Exact arrival and departure dates.
4. The reason for your stay, written in the transitional lease.

## Do I pay the tourist tax?

Not with a registered monthly lease. On per-night bookings, such as the Precotto apartment, the Milan tourist tax is €9.50 per adult per night for the first 14 nights.
