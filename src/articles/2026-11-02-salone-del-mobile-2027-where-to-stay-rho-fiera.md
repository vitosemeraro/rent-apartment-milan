Where should you stay for **Salone del Mobile 2027**? Choose a flat on the **M1 red line**: trains bound for Rho Fiera run with no changes, so you board near your door and get off at the fair. Our Precotto apartment sleeps up to 8 and starts at €85 per night, which makes it easy to split between a stand crew.

## Which metro line goes to Rho Fiera?

The **M1 red line**. Precotto and Turro are both on it. Take a train in the direction of **Rho Fiera Milano** (not every train goes to the end of the line, so check the destination on the display). You do not change at Duomo, Cadorna or anywhere else.

In the evening the same line takes you back to Cadorna, Duomo and Corso Buenos Aires. The Duomo is about 20 minutes from our stops.

## When is Salone del Mobile 2027?

The fair is held in **April**, together with Milan Design Week in the city. The exact dates change each year and are published by the organiser, so check the official website before you book. If you already know your dates, the booking calendar on each apartment page shows whether they are free.

## Where can you stay for the fair?

| Option | Good for | Things to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hotel near Rho | One person, one night | Prices rise sharply in fair week, and you often have no kitchen |
| Hotel in the centre | Evenings out | Metro commute to the fair, high rates |
| Apartment on the M1 | Teams, 3+ nights | Direct metro, kitchen, shared cost |

An apartment is not always cheaper for one person on one night. It becomes a strong choice for **groups and longer stays**, where you split the price and use the kitchen for breakfast.

The Milan tourist tax is €9.50 per adult per night for the first 14 nights; under-18s are exempt.

## How early should you book?

Design Week is the busiest week of the Milan year. Start looking **four to six months ahead**, and send a request as soon as you know the dates. Fair weeks and events can carry higher rates, and the best-placed flats go first.

## What should you pack or check before arrival?

1. Confirm the fair's opening hours and entry ticket on the organiser's website.
2. Ask for check-in details: arrival is self check-in.
3. Plan your first trip to Rho Fiera on a weekday morning to see how busy the M1 is.
4. Keep a contact for questions: I answer messages myself.
