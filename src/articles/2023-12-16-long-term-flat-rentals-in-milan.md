Whether you are moving to Milan for work, study or a new chapter of life, a long-term furnished flat lets you settle in without buying furniture or signing a classic 4+4 lease.

## What "long term" means in Milan

- **1 to 18 months**: a [transitional lease](/transitional-lease-contract-milan/), the most common contract for assignments, internships and semesters.
- **6 to 36 months** for university students: the student lease.
- **4 years and more**: the classic open-market lease, usually unfurnished.

## Why furnished and all-inclusive

Activating electricity, gas and internet in Italy takes time and paperwork. With a furnished flat **with bills included** you arrive with a suitcase and start living from day one, with one predictable monthly cost.

## Where to look

North-east Milan along the **M1 red line** offers a good balance: residential and green, close to Bicocca and Niguarda, with a direct line to the centre and to Rho Fiera.
